Scene 1 / 12 — Introduction
RSEA Future-State Entitlements Workflow
Entitlements are per-role purchasing allowances — a budget or quantity of specific stock that company employees can claim against their employer's account. Pronto Xi is master; App Builder caches the rules; Adobe Commerce enforces them at PDP, cart and checkout.
1
📋

Define in Pronto

RSEA staff create the entitlement header, add stock-code lines with qty / spend limits, and assign a cycle.

▶
2
🔄

Sync via App Builder

Woven pushes entitlement_rules to App Builder. Pronto remains source of truth.

▶
3
🛒

Redeem in Commerce

Webhook validates each line item; balance shown on PDP; discount applied at line-item level.

📖 Worked example used through this walkthrough: BBP Constructions — Full Time Employee role is entitled to:
  • 1 pair of work boots every 24 months, capped at $200
  • 4 work shirts every 12 months, capped at $60 each

TRANSITION
Woven syncs the three Pronto custom objects (header, lines, claim details) into App Builder's entitlement_rules table on PlanetScale. App Builder becomes the low-latency rules cache that Adobe Commerce queries at PDP, cart and checkout. Flow is not one-way — zcus-ecommerce-user goes the other direction: Adobe Commerce is master for company users, so user records sync back to Pronto so it can resolve who's claiming against which entitlement.

STEP 6 — Rules Cached
Inside App Builder, the entitlement_rules table on PlanetScale holds the flattened rule set Woven pushes from Pronto. Each Pronto entitlement line becomes one or more rule rows scoped by account_code + role_id + emp_status. Adobe Commerce reads from here — never directly from Pronto.

STEP 7 — PDP Balance Lookup
When Sarah (BBP, FT Employee) opens this boot product page, Adobe Commerce calls the App Builder webhook. The webhook matches her role to entitlement_rules, checks current usage, and returns the remaining balance — shown as a banner on the PDP, with a no returns notice for entitlement-claimed items. Because Sarah's role permits purchasing on behalf of others, a Purchase for selector also renders alongside the product options; when an employee other than herself is selected, the cart line is tagged with their customer_id and validated against their entitlement at checkout.

STEP 8 — Checkout Enforcement
At checkout, Adobe Commerce re-runs the entitlement validation webhook on every cart line. Because the $189 boots fall fully within Sarah's $200 allowance, the line gets a 100% discount_amount applied via additional_data, the order total drops to $0, and the only payment option offered is No Payment Method. The redemption is logged to entitlement_usage at Pending status.

End-to-End Flow — Recap
Three systems, one entitlement. Each system has clear ownership and clear handoffs — Pronto authors, App Builder caches, Adobe Commerce enforces.
SOURCE OF TRUTH

🏢 Pronto Xi (Woven)

  • Maintain Entitlements — header (debtor / role / emp status / cycle)
  • Entitlement Lines — sku, style or group with max qty & max spend
  • Users (read-only — sourced back from Adobe Commerce)
  • Nightly Period Roll — automated cycle reset
  • Receives claim records back, marks as Claimed when order is updated
INTEGRATION LAYER

⚡ App Builder + PlanetScale

  • entitlement_rules — flattened rule rows by role & sku
  • entitlement_usage — pending & claimed redemptions
  • Webhook endpoint called by Adobe Commerce at PDP, cart & checkout
  • 15-minute TTL cache · scoped by website_id
  • Owned and pushed by Woven; rules never authored here
CUSTOMER ENFORCEMENT

🅐 Adobe Commerce

  • PDP balance banner — "remaining qty / $" for the logged-in user
  • Cart & checkout validation — line-item discount_amount
  • "No Payment Method" when fully covered; CC / PO when excess
  • My Entitlements page in customer dashboard
  • Order splits when purchase on-behalf-of multiple employees
Several alignment gaps between the Pronto and Adobe specs remain to be resolved before build — see next.
Alignment Gaps — Pronto (Woven) vs Adobe (Acidgreen) Specs
Eleven places where the two Confluence specs describe the same workflow differently, are silent on the other side, or use a different data shape. Resolve the HIGH items before build; the others can be confirmed during integration.
High — must resolve Medium — confirm contract Low — record & monitor
HIGH · 1 Data model shape
Pronto · Woven Three tables: entitlement_header, entitlement_lines, claim_details.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen Two tables: entitlement_rules, entitlement_usage. Granularity isn't 1:1 — transformation rules from 3→2 are not documented.
HIGH · 2 Where claims are authored
Pronto · Woven Claims screen is read-only — records originate in Adobe Commerce and are surfaced back to Pronto.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen Usage records created in Commerce as temporary, then confirmed/reversed by Pronto. Field-by-field reconciliation undocumented; PO-rejection reversal appears in Adobe spec only.
MED · 3 Cycle & period model
Pronto · Woven Cycle lives on the header: Start Cycle, End Cycle, Expiry. Nightly Period Roll resets balances automatically.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen Cycle lives on the line: frequency + start_date. No expiry concept.
MED · 4 Frequency units
Pronto · Woven Expressed as date ranges (e.g. 01-Jan-26 → 31-Dec-26).
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen Expressed as durations (e.g. "24mo", "12mo"). App Builder must derive one from the other.
MED · 5 Stock targeting fields
Pronto · Woven Three target fields: stock_code, style_code, stock_group. Style and group expand into many SKUs via zcus-catalogue-line-stock.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen One freeform SKU/identifier field on each rule. Expansion logic owned by App Builder; rules and reconciliation undocumented.
MED · 6 Purchase-on-behalf scope
Pronto · Woven Claims screen records the entitled wearer; no per-purchaser-vs-wearer distinction.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen Detailed flow: purchased_for_customer_id, order splitting per wearer, role-based permissions on the "Purchase For" dropdown.
MED · 8 Excess-payment method
Pronto · Woven Assumes a mapped payment method exists; no role-based rules in spec.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen Specifies Credit Card vs Purchase Order based on role permissions, with PO approval workflow.
LOW · 7 Bulk CSV purchase on-behalf
Pronto · Woven Not mentioned.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen CSV bulk-order flow described for site managers placing orders across multiple wearers in one go.
LOW · 9 My Entitlements page
Pronto · Woven Not mentioned.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen Customer dashboard page proposed (still listed as an open question — #4 — in Adobe spec).
LOW · 10 PO rejection reversal
Pronto · Woven Silent on cleanup when a PO is later rejected.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen Adobe-internal cleanup specified — pending entitlement_usage rows must be reversed. Possible sync edge case if Pronto already saw the claim.
LOW · 11 Cart-level entitlement
Pronto · Woven Out of scope.
vs
Adobe · Acidgreen Out of scope. Both agree — recorded here so it isn't reintroduced as a request later.
Must-resolve before build: #1 Data model #2 Claims authoring #5 Stock targeting
