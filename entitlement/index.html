Entitlement Workflow
via Commerce Vision

Commerce Vision → Pronto Xi → Customer

Operator builds entitlement rules and per-role limits in the Commerce Vision CMS, with claims tracked against the user account. Overnight reconciliation pushes redemption data into Pronto for invoicing.

To-Be · Future State

Entitlement Workflow
via Adobe Commerce

Pronto Xi → App Builder → Adobe Commerce → Customer

Pronto Xi becomes the master. Operator creates the entitlement header, defines stock-code / style / group lines with qty & spend caps, and the rules sync through App Builder's PlanetScale cache. Adobe Commerce enforces them on PDP, cart and checkout — applying line-item discounts and surfacing "No Payment Method" when an order is fully covered.
